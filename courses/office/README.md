# Office test course (Tim's copy only, not in Mark's package)

A made-up 4-hole course on the paved apron northwest of the office building,
for walk-testing the GPS app, yardages, auto-advance, the 3D view and (with
care) the trolley without driving to Tashua. Built 2026-10-28.

The property has essentially no lawn, so the "course" is asphalt: the open
apron between the tree line (I-95 beyond it) and the building's northwest
face, plus the lane along the building's north end. See `data/layout_check.jpg`
(outline magenta, tees white, greens green, OSM buildings orange, 20 m grid).

| Hole | Par | Yards | Runs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3 | 41 | SW corner by the trailer yard, up the tree side |
| 2 | 3 | 40 | top of the apron, east along the north lane |
| 3 | 3 | 84 | north lane, back diagonally down past the building face |
| 4 | 3 | 56 | south lane, back up the middle |

Greens are 8 m across (4 m radius). The "Switch to hole N?" banner is
tightened for this course: it appears within 8 m of the next tee (Tashua uses
25 m). (`green_m` is no longer used.)

## Files

- `layout.py`: the layout in local meters from the building's west corner.
  **Edit this** to move holes, then run the rebuild below.
- `course-def.json`: generated by `layout.py` (lat/lon); read by `make_test_course.py`.
- `data/holes.gpx`: tees and pins as waypoints, to find them on a phone.
- `data/osm_map.json`: the OSM download for the area (buildings, roads, parking).
- `data/terrain_check.png`, `data/layout_check.jpg`: checks to look at after a rebuild.

## Run it

```bash
python server/gps_server.py --course office --sim --mock-trolley --review   # PC; /review.html for routes
```

Preview entry: `golf-office` (port 8076) in `C:\.claude\launch.json`. On the Pi, set
`GOLF_COURSE=office` (`sudo systemctl edit golf-map`) and copy `courses/` over.

Rebuild after editing `layout.py` (from the project root):

```bash
python courses/office/layout.py
python tools/make_test_course.py --course office
python tools/build_terrain.py --course office --check
python tools/build_routes.py --course office
```

`fetch_aerial` / `fetch_elevation` only need rerunning if the outline moves a lot.
**Watch the trolley grid**: greens block 7 m around the pin (4 m green + 3 m
buffer) and the apron is only ~30 m wide. If two greens sit side by side the
north half becomes unreachable (lots of code 9 "can't drive to" cells, and
`build_routes` reports NO ROUTE). The first draft did exactly that.

## Field test checklist

- [ ] Pi (or laptop) with the USB GPS and gpsd; check `GPS ±` settles to under 5 yd.
- [ ] Load `data/holes.gpx` on a phone; stand on each tee and pin and compare the app's yardage to a laser or paced distance.
- [ ] Walk the loop: at the hole 2 tee the screen asks "Switch to hole 2?"; Switch changes hole, Not now hides it until you walk away. Repeat for each hole.
- [ ] 3D button and flyovers; note the frame rate (first real Pi renderer test).
- [ ] Tap-to-measure on a couple of known points (building corner, tank).
- [ ] Mock trolley only (`--mock-trolley`). The real trolley only off-hours, with a spotter holding the stop, and with the building/landlord's OK. Trucks, trailers and parked cars are **not** on any map; the geofence and heartbeat stop are the only protection.

## Known limits

- The open roof structure north of the lane isn't in OSM; the outline keeps
  ~4 m off it by hand (from the photo), so the keep-out map doesn't know it's there.
- The OSM drive aisle (`highway=service`) runs through the apron and is left
  drivable on purpose; public roads (Honeyspot Rd, I-95) are 50+ m outside the fence.
- One proposed route, `h04-tee-green`, is flagged "long way round (1.9×)": it
  detours around hole 1's green. All 8 routes are still "proposed".
- Lidar is bare-earth, so in 3D the buildings are flat photo on the ground.
